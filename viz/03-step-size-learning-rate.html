How big a step? — the "learning rate"

Same valley (the error). The slope tells the ball which way is downhill. The step-size slider decides how far it walks each step. Crank it up and watch what happens.

The orange dots are the ball's past positions — the trail shows whether it glides in, bounces across the bottom, or flies apart.

The update each step is literally: knob = knob − (step size) × slope. Small step → slow crawl. Too-big step → leap over the bottom and overshoot. Way-too-big → each leap lands farther out and the error explodes. There's a sweet spot in between — finding it is a real part of the job in ML.
