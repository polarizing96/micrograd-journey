Read the picture: the green triangle is your nudge — you step right by h (the run) and the curve steps up or down by Δy (the rise). Slope = rise / run. That's the dashed green line's steepness.

Now the "calculus" part — just drag h toward 0. Watch the green dashed line swing until it lies right on top of the red line, and watch Δy / h settle onto the gradient number. A derivative is nothing more than the slope you get when the nudge becomes infinitely tiny. No mystery — it's the wiggle ratio after you stop shrinking.

Why this is the whole game in micrograd: every Value you build will carry a .grad — and .grad is exactly this red number: "if I nudge me a hair, how much does the final output move, and which way?" Backprop is just computing this slope for every knob at once.
