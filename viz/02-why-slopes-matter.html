Why the slope matters

This curve is the error — lower is better. The red ball is where your knob is set. Press the button and watch the slope roll it downhill to the lowest error. That is training.

Read it: the green arrow shows the slope under the ball. Slope positive (arrow points up-right) → downhill is LEFT, so step left. Slope negative (arrow points up-left) → step RIGHT. Either way: step the opposite way the slope points.

The rule each step: knob = knob − (small number) × slope. The minus sign is what makes it go downhill. When the slope reaches ~0 the ball is at the bottom: the error can't drop any more, so it stops. That's the whole training loop.

In micrograd: a real net has thousands of these knobs (weights). Backprop computes the slope for all of them at once, then every knob takes one step downhill. Repeat → the error falls → the net learns.
